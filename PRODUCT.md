# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, technical reviewers, and potential collaborators evaluating Catur Setyo Ragil's real backend, data, AI, and web work.

## Product Purpose

Present Catur as an IT student, backend developer, and data & AI builder through inspectable projects, clear contributions, and direct contact paths.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, candid, and precise. The experience should feel calm and personal while giving the work more weight than decorative effects.

## Anti-references

Do not clone akryst.moe, use its assets or identity, cover the site in glass panels, rely on generic gradient decoration, or make experience claims that the projects do not support.

## Design Principles

- Let real project evidence lead.
- Use one strong visual atmosphere across the long scroll.
- Keep metadata quiet and primary content readable.
- Use motion to orient, never to hide.
- Preserve familiar navigation and accessible interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic HTML, full keyboard access, visible focus, readable mobile layouts, and a complete reduced-motion experience.
